How to use this calculator

  • Enter your current pregnancy week (the week your doctor gave you), and the day if you know it.
  • Tap Convert to months — you'll see your exact month and how many weeks remain in that month.
  • Pregnancy months are not calendar months: each one is exactly 4 weeks (28 days).

Why pregnancy is 10 "months," not 9

A full pregnancy = 40 weeks = 10 months of 4 weeks

Pregnancy weeks are counted from the first day of your last period, which is about two weeks before conception. Dividing 40 weeks by 4 gives 10 pregnancy months. Because calendar months are a few days longer than 28 days, those 10 pregnancy months add up to roughly 9 calendar months — which is why people say "9 months" while the medical chart says 40 weeks.

Common questions

How many weeks is 4 months pregnant?▾
In pregnancy dating, one month is counted as 4 weeks. So 4 months pregnant means around week 16 of pregnancy.
How many weeks is 5 months pregnant?▾
5 months pregnant is about week 20 of pregnancy. Since each pregnancy month is 4 weeks, multiply months by 4.
How many weeks is 6 months pregnant?▾
6 months pregnant is about week 24. You are well into the second trimester at this point.
How many weeks is 7 months pregnant?▾
7 months pregnant is about week 28. This is the start of the third trimester.
Why is pregnancy 10 months instead of 9?▾
Pregnancy is counted from the first day of your last period, about two weeks before conception. A full 40-week pregnancy equals 10 months of 4 weeks each, which is roughly 9 calendar months.
