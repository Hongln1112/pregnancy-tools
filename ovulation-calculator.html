How to use this calculator

  • Enter your average cycle length in days (a typical cycle is 28 days).
  • Select the first day of your last period.
  • Tap Calculate fertile window to see your most likely ovulation day and the best days to try to conceive.

How it works

Ovulation happens about 14 days before your next period

That means for a 28-day cycle, ovulation is around day 14. Your fertile window is roughly the 5 days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation, because sperm can survive in the body for several days.

Cycles vary, and this calculator is most accurate for regular cycles. If your cycle is irregular, tracking other signs — or talking to a doctor — can help more.

Common questions

When do I ovulate?▾
Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before your next period. For a 28-day cycle that is around day 14; for longer or shorter cycles it shifts accordingly.
How long is my fertile window?▾
Your fertile window is roughly the 5 days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation — about 6 days in total — because sperm can survive in the body for several days.
Is this calculator accurate for irregular cycles?▾
It is most accurate for regular cycles. If your cycle is irregular, tracking other signs or talking to a doctor can help more.
