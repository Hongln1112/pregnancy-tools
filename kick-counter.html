How to use the kick counter

  • Pick a time when your baby is usually active, sit quietly or lie on your side, and tap I felt a kick! every time you feel movement.
  • Continue until you've counted 10 movements or for about 2 hours, whichever comes first.
  • The session shows your total, how long you've been counting, and your pace per 10 minutes.

What's a healthy pattern?

A common guide: at least 10 movements within 2 hours

Every baby has its own rhythm — some are more active in the morning, others at night. What matters most is that your baby's pattern doesn't clearly drop from what's normal for you. From around 28 weeks, movements become more established, which is when most providers suggest counting regularly.

Common questions

How many kicks are normal?▾
A common guideline is that your baby should move at least 10 times within 2 hours — the 'count-to-ten' method. Every baby has their own pattern, so get to know yours and contact your care team if movement drops.
When should I start counting kicks?▾
Most care providers suggest tracking movements regularly from around 28 weeks, when movement patterns become more established.
What should I do if I feel fewer movements?▾
Try a quiet session, lie on your side, and count for a while. If you still notice clearly fewer movements than usual, contact your doctor or midwife right away — never wait it out.
