How to use this calculator

  • Select your baby's birth date.
  • Tap Calculate baby's age — you'll see their age in months, plus a breakdown in weeks and days.
  • Revisit it anytime — at appointments, for feeding schedules, or just to marvel at how fast they're growing.

Why it matters

Doctors track development by age, and knowing the exact weeks and months helps you understand growth milestones, feeding schedules, and when to expect certain behaviors. This simple tool gives you a quick reference anytime.

Common questions

How is my baby's age calculated?▾
The tool compares today's date to your baby's birth date, showing their age in months, weeks, and days. After 12 months the age is also shown in years.
Why is it helpful to know my baby's age in weeks?▾
Doctors track development by age, and many feeding schedules, sleep patterns, and growth milestones are referenced in weeks and months rather than years.
When should I use months vs. weeks?▾
In the first year, age is usually described in weeks and months. After 12 months it becomes more natural to talk in years and months.
