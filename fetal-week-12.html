Week 12 of pregnancy

Week 12 — A complete little baby

This week your baby is about the size of a lime.

What's happening this week

  • By now your baby is a complete little person — arms, legs, fingers and toes are all formed.
  • The face is clear, with eyes, nose and mouth well defined.
  • Tiny fingernails begin to form, and external genitalia start to develop.
  • The first trimester screening ultrasound is usually offered around this time.
Source: Mayo Clinic, Prenatal care in your first trimester. Learn more

Questions about week 12

When is the first ultrasound?▾
The first trimester screening ultrasound is usually offered between 11 and 13 weeks. It checks development and helps confirm your due date.
Is my baby fully formed now?▾
The main body structures are complete. From here your baby mostly grows and matures rather than forming new parts — the risk of many complications drops after this point.
