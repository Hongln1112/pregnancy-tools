Why we built this

Pregnancy comes with a thousand questions — and you shouldn't need an app, an account, or a subscription to get quick, reliable answers. Our calculators estimate due dates, pregnancy weeks, ovulation windows, healthy weight gain, fetal size, and more in seconds, right in your browser.

Our guides explain common symptoms, nutrition, prenatal tests and birth preparation in plain language, so you can go into each appointment informed and calm.

Private by design

  • All calculations run locally in your browser — your dates and numbers never leave your device.
  • No account, no email, no sign-up.
  • Free to use. That's the whole point.

Where our information comes from

Our calculators and guides follow standard, widely accepted medical guidelines — the same formulas most healthcare providers use, such as Naegele's Rule for due dates and the common 40-week dating method. Growth ranges, trimester definitions, checkup timings and symptom guidance reflect general obstetric practice and authoritative sources, cross-checked for consistency.

Important: Everything here is for general information and is not a substitute for professional medical care. Every pregnancy is different. Always confirm details — due dates, scans, tests, nutrition — with your doctor or midwife.
